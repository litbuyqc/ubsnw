02 / The quick answer

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links, categories and comparison notes. A useful list records the source URL, selected variant, measurements and the date checked. It helps you shortlist products; it does not independently verify stock, seller reliability or authenticity. This page provides a browsing guide and category links rather than a live inventory or downloadable spreadsheet.

04 / The comparison desk

Give every saved link a little context.

Use this Oopbuy shopping guide as a reference for your own comparison sheet. Keep unknown information marked as unknown until you can verify it.

Suggested fields for a product comparison spreadsheet
Field What to record Why it helps
Source & variant Product URL, seller name, color, size and date checked. Keeps similar listings and variants from getting mixed up.
Measurements Relevant garment dimensions, shoe sizing notes or accessory dimensions. Supports comparisons with an item you already own.
Materials & photos Seller-provided composition, construction details and available images. Helps identify missing information and questions to ask.
Cost estimate Item price, currency, delivery, service fees and applicable import charges. Makes the likely total more visible than the item price alone.
Order conditions Current availability, return terms and service acceptance requirements. Highlights restrictions before payment.
Budget reference: item cost + domestic delivery + service fees + international shipping + applicable import charges. Check which amounts are included in a quote before adding them together.

05 / From discovery to decision

A calmer way to compare your finds.

Before saving an item

Make sure the listing answers the basic questions for your chosen category.

  • Choose the exact color and size you intend to compare.
  • Check whether the images show that selected variant.
  • Record measurements and note any missing material details.
  • Save the source link rather than relying on a cropped screenshot.

Before placing an order

Recheck information that may have changed since you first saved the link.

  • Confirm current stock, price and return conditions.
  • Check the service’s current ordering rules and product restrictions.
  • Review the full cost estimate for your delivery destination.
  • Use any available inspection photos to review visible details; photos alone do not prove authenticity.

07 / Useful answers

Oopbuy spreadsheet questions, answered.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links, categories and comparison notes. A list can help you shortlist items, but it does not independently verify stock, seller reliability or authenticity.

Is this the official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent category and shopping guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS. It does not represent Oopbuy or any featured brand.

How should I compare spreadsheet finds?

Compare the source listing, selected variant, measurements, material description, available product photos and return conditions. Save the product URL and the date you checked it.

Does a listed price include delivery and import charges?

Do not assume it does. Check item cost, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any applicable import charges separately before paying.

Do the brand illustrations confirm product availability or authenticity?

No. The illustrations are editorial graphics, not product photographs. They do not confirm availability, authenticity, brand approval or a relationship between a brand and CAFANS.

Can a spreadsheet guarantee that an item is compatible with Oopbuy?

No. Check the current ordering requirements and product restrictions directly with the service before placing an order. A third-party list cannot guarantee that a listing will be accepted.
